# Department of David Purvis — Creative Brief

A separate creative brief was not supplied with the handoff. This one is drawn only from the
handoff's §1 and §2 (see [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md)), so it adds nothing the handoff doesn't say.

## The premise

**Opening David’s GitHub requires administrative oversight, and the Department sincerely believes
this is appropriate.**

The Department of David Purvis is a solemn public-service institution. It performs elaborate
procedures around the ordinary task of learning about David. The comedy is the main objective.
The professional résumé is the abrupt, ordinary ending.

## Audience and success

- **Audience:** everyone, including nontechnical visitors.
- **Success:** visitors enjoy discovering the site and want to share it.

## What stays fixed

- **Exploration:** any order. There are no prerequisites, completion scores or mandatory
  sequences.
- **Attractions:** the existing ones keep their depth and working machinery.
- **The résumé:** can be requested at any time, and there is always an ordinary direct
  alternative. It ends the performance: accurate, professionally formatted, and free of
  institutional material.

## Voice

The Department uses concrete administrative verbs: *received, recorded, referred, reviewed,
retained, returned, approved.*

1. Explain procedures, not the comedy.
2. Become calmer as the decision becomes less reasonable.
3. Keep action labels understandable.
4. Give each interaction one dominant premise.
5. Leave space after its payoff.
6. Describe accessibility, errors, storage and exits plainly.
7. Understate David while the institution supplies the grandeur.

**Never write:** "the house is rigged", "verifies nothing", "just kidding" or "this page
contains no jokes". Factual technical explanations belong in Facilities.

## Look

- **Palette and type:** paper, navy and restrained red. IBM Plex Sans for interface text, IBM Plex
  Mono for reference numbers.
- **Structure:** thin rules, service rows, document sections, restrained borders.
- **Avoid:** rounded promotional cards, oversized slogans, decorative clutter.
- **Minimums:**
  - body text at least 16px, routine labels at least 14px;
  - 44px targets, visible focus;
  - a usable dark presentation and reduced-motion behavior.
- **Attractions:** each keeps its own visual language inside the shared shell.

## Opening

> **Department of David Purvis**
> Public access to information concerning David Purvis.
>
> **Subject:** David Purvis
> **Capability:** Good at computers.

After the opening come:

- a six-category directory;
- **Request résumé**;
- a stationary **Go directly to résumé**;
- a compact case-status line.

Nothing opens, moves focus, loads third-party media or starts an attraction on arrival.

## Directory

| Category | Services |
|---|---|
| Visitor Services | Classification, verification |
| Records | Character Review, Personnel File, Skills, Projects, Newsletter, résumé selection |
| Correspondence | Contact instruments, hyperlink allocation |
| Public Affairs | Beliefs, causes, wishlist, public statements, self-assessment forms |
| Recreation | DOOM, cube, presentation, HUD, gameplay modes, musical material |
| Facilities | Display settings, cookie administration, privacy, credits, technical documentation |

## Small results (the payoffs)

| Department | The result |
|---|---|
| Classification | "Noted." or "Declaration of nondeclaration received." |
| Verification | "Review complete." |
| Character Review | The shopping-cart finding: "Cart returned." |
| Hyperlink allocation | "The requested destination is available. Allocation is pending." A failure reads "Your request remains within the processing period." Success is "Open GitHub" (or the destination requested). |
| Correspondence | "Address prepared. Nothing was sent." |
| Terms of reading | "I have read this sentence." leads to "Reading acknowledged." |
| Release | "Approved." |

## The tribute

The Terry A. Davis tribute is sincere. The institution does not operate there: no status line,
notices, advertisements, title changes, novelty themes or overlays.

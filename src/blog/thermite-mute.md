My Rainbow Six Siege handle is Sasquatch.- (the period and the hyphen are load-bearing), and I play two operators more than any others: Thermite on attack and Mute on defense.

Recruiters love to ask about teamwork. Allow me to explain mine in terms of exothermic charges.

## Thermite: open the wall, let the team win

Thermite's whole job is to burn a hole through a reinforced wall so the rest of the team can get in. He rarely gets the highlight clip. He gets the round.

At my last job I was the sole technical resource for a system used across 22 departments. That is Thermite work. You open the wall, everyone walks through, and nobody remembers who brought the charges. That's fine. The round is what counts.

## Mute: jam the noise before it finds anyone

Mute's gadgets jam drones and electronics so the enemy can't gather information on his teammates. He protects people from things they never see coming.

In real life, I mute distractions: the meeting that could have been an email, the scope creep that could have been a ticket, the reply-all that could have been nothing.

## Still carrying my weight

There is a type of support player who breaches, jams, and then contributes nothing else to the round. The team chat has words for him. I am not him. I open the wall, and then I go through it too.

## Transferable skills, formally stated

- Gives clear callouts under pressure.
- Reinforces the walls that matter (documentation).
- Doesn't peek when he doesn't have to.
- Plays the objective, not the scoreboard.
- Will absolutely clutch a 1v3 and then pretend it was nothing.

There were two sign-up sheets. One was for the swim meet. The other was to name the goldfish.

I signed one of them. It was the wrong one.

## What happened next

The fish was named in my honor.

I did not swim in the meet. I have never swum competitively. The fish, famously, swam every day, competitively against no one, and won every time.

## Lessons learned

- **Read the header of any form before you sign it.** This lesson later made me very good at reviewing contracts.
- **Legacy is unpredictable.** Some people get buildings named after them. I got a fish.
- **You can be part of a team without being on the team.** In high-school aquatics, I was not an athlete. I was the brand.

## Where is the fish now?

The Department has not been able to confirm the fish's current whereabouts, and we have decided not to ask follow-up questions.
